Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Reading this site

This is a static information site hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added advertising, visitor analytics, accounts or our own tracking cookies. The hosting provider processes connection information when you visit; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Feedback using Google Forms

Our feedback and topic-request page links to a separate Google Form. If you submit it, the answers you choose to provide are handled by Google and made available to the site operator to review feedback, correct errors and plan topics. The form is optional. Google may use cookies or account features under Google’s privacy policy.

Do not send passwords, identification numbers, full addresses, bank information, medical details or confidential documents. Please provide only the information needed to explain your question. For a privacy concern or a request about a previous response, use the same feedback form with enough non-sensitive context to identify it. Do not resend sensitive details.

Links and changes

Other websites have their own privacy practices. We will update this page if this site’s data handling changes.
