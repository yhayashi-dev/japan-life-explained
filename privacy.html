Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Reading this site

This is a static information site hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added advertising or accounts. Optional visitor analytics and its cookies are described below; measurement runs only after you consent. The hosting provider processes connection information when you visit; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Feedback using Google Forms

Our feedback and topic-request page links to a separate Google Form. If you submit it, the answers you choose to provide are handled by Google and made available to the site operator to review feedback, correct errors and plan topics. The form is optional. Google may use cookies or account features under Google’s privacy policy.

Feedback by email

The feedback page also provides an email address hosted by Gmail. Email is optional. If you send email, your sender address, message and any information you include are sent to the site operator’s Gmail mailbox. Your own email provider and the receiving provider handle the message. The operator uses feedback to correct errors and plan topics. See Google’s privacy policy for the receiving service.

Do not send passwords, identification numbers, full addresses, bank information, medical details or confidential documents. Please provide only the information needed to explain your question. For a privacy concern or a request about a previous response, use one of the channels on the feedback page with enough non-sensitive context to identify it. Do not resend sensitive details.

Links and changes

Other websites have their own privacy practices. We will update this page if this site’s data handling changes.

Optional analytics and cookies

Optional visitor measurement with the standard free version of Google Analytics 4 is available from 16 October 2026 (Japan time), only after you consent. It measures page views, estimated browsers and sessions, and safe source categories only for browsers that consent on this site. These figures do not represent all visitors or a count of actual people.

Only after consent, Google (including googletagmanager.com and google-analytics.com) receives fixed approved public-page URLs and titles, visit time, safe source categories and registered campaigns, basic browser/device information and pseudonymous client/session identifiers. Site-specific consent cookies and analytics cookies are used. Your IP address reaches Google during transmission and may be used for regional processing.

Analytics does not receive your inputs, search terms, support conditions, income, medical details or results, FGO ownership/training/teams/localStorage/JSON, inquiry content or email addresses. URL queries and fragments, raw referrer paths/queries and arbitrary campaign values are excluded. Advertising, Google Signals, User-ID and enhanced measurement are not used.

Before consent or after rejection, no Google analytics scripts or measurement requests are sent. You can use the site without consenting. Use the persistent analytics settings button to change or withdraw consent for this site at any time. Withdrawal stops measurement and deletes this site’s analytics cookies; it does not undo data already sent.

Visits may not be captured where Google is unavailable, including mainland China, or when consent is declined, requests are blocked, or blockers are used. Simplified Chinese readers are not necessarily in mainland China. Google search traffic and Chinese-language readership are different; no recorded visit does not mean no actual visit.

Measurement is optional and requires consent. In addition to the previously allowlisted English, Vietnamese and Simplified Chinese pages, the new five Nepali and five Indonesian articles, the language-selection page, and the Nepali and Indonesian guide indexes are included. The five Burmese articles and all other newly added language-specific search and common-information pages remain outside measurement. Search terms and feedback inputs are never sent to analytics.
