Mail in Japan: Forwarding and Missed Deliveries

Last checked: October 9, 2026 — Japan Post forwarding and receiving procedures.

What do you need to do?

  • Moving within Japan? Arrange mail forwarding with Japan Post.
  • Found a missed-delivery notice? Check the carrier, deadline and receiving options.

This guide covers Japan Post. Other delivery companies have their own procedures. Postal forwarding is separate from registering your address at your municipality; one does not replace the other.

Moving: arrange forwarding

Japan Post’s forwarding service is free. Submit a relocation notice (転居届, tenkyo todoke) at a post office counter, by posting the paper form, or through e-Tenkyo online. Check the current application and identity-check instructions (Japanese) for your chosen method. Online applications require an identity-verified Yu ID.

Have the old and new addresses, the names of people moving and your requested forwarding start date ready. Apply early: Japan Post says registration takes 3–7 business days.

The forwarding period is one year from the date you submit the notice, not your requested start date. See the English forwarding overview. Use the current application instructions above for accepted identification documents.

Forwarding does not update every sender

Tell banks, employers and other important senders your new address directly. Items marked 転送不要 (tensō fuyō, do not forward) are not forwarded even if you have submitted a relocation notice. See Japan Post’s non-forwarding explanation (Japanese).

Moving overseas? Domestic mail is not forwarded to an overseas address. Check Japan Post’s overseas-moving guidance (Japanese) and arrange delivery with your senders.

Missed a delivery: use the notice

  1. Check the delivery company. Use that company’s official instructions. A notice from another carrier cannot be handled through Japan Post’s form.
  2. Keep the notice and check its deadline. Look for the tracking or inquiry number, responsible office and holding deadline (保管期限, hokan kigen). Japan Post’s general FAQ (Japanese) says items missed during an absence are held for seven days, then returned if not delivered within that period. Check your notice and contact the office promptly if the deadline is unclear or has passed.
  3. Choose redelivery or ask about collection. Follow the official route for your item; do not assume all items have the same receiving options.

Redelivery to your home

Have your notice ready and open Japan Post’s English redelivery form. This form is for redelivery to the home address written on the notice. For another location or collection at a post office, use the contact instructions on that page. Available dates and times depend on the service.

Collecting at a post office

Confirm the collection office and its opening hours before going. Japan Post’s collection guidance (Japanese) lists the notice and identification showing the recipient’s name and address, plus a signature or seal. Special receiving services have different requirements, so check the instructions for your item. Do not assume any identification document or any post office will do.

Words you may see

  • ご不在連絡票 (go-fuzai renrakuhyō): missed-delivery notice.
  • 再配達 (saihaitatsu): redelivery.
  • 転居届 (tenkyo todoke): relocation notice here means Japan Post’s form. Your municipality’s address-change procedure is separate.

For help understanding a notice, contact the responsible carrier. For help finding local language support, see Find local help; a consultation desk does not arrange delivery for you.
