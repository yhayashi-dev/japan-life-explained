We explain everyday procedures in plain English. Official rules and instructions come first. We prefer government, municipal and other public-agency sources; service-provider rules may also be needed for bank, phone or rental questions.

A simple guide is not a complete statement of the law. Rules can vary by personal situation. A last-checked date means we reviewed that page on that date, not that we monitor it in real time.
